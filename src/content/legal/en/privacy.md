## Who is responsible

Francisco José Bas Guil. You can write to me at [franbasguil@gmail.com](mailto:franbasguil@gmail.com) about anything to do with your data.

## What data I collect and why

What you type into the contact form: your name, your email address and your message. I use them only to reply to you. I do not use them for anything else or share them with anyone.

I also count visits anonymously: see “Visit statistics”.

## Who handles the form

The form is handled by Netlify, the service that hosts this website. Messages are stored in my Netlify account until I delete them.

## How long I keep it

As long as I need to reply to you and continue the conversation. If you ask me to, I will delete it sooner.

## Your rights

You can ask me at any time what data I hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, by writing to the email address above.

## Visit statistics

To know how many people visit the website and which pages they read, I use GoatCounter, a statistics service without cookies. For each visit it receives the page, the website you came from, your browser, your operating system, your screen size and your country. It uses your IP address only to work out the country and to avoid counting the same visit twice for a few hours, and it does not store it. Nothing is stored in your browser either. I only see totals, never an individual person. The data is stored on servers in the European Union (Finland and Germany).

## Cookies

This website uses no cookies. The only external script is GoatCounter's, and it is served from this website.
