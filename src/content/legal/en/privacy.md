## Who is responsible

Francisco José Bas Guil. You can write to me at [franbasguil@gmail.com](mailto:franbasguil@gmail.com) about anything to do with your data.

## What data I collect and why

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address and your message. I use them only to reply to you. I do not use them for anything else or share them with anyone.

## Who handles the form

The form is handled by Netlify, the service that hosts this website. Messages are stored in my Netlify account until I delete them.

## How long I keep it

As long as I need to reply to you and continue the conversation. If you ask me to, I will delete it sooner.

## Your rights

You can ask me at any time what data I hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, by writing to the email address above.

## Cookies and analytics

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.
